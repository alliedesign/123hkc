Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Home Key Clean ("we", "us") runs HomeKeyClean.com, a marketplace that connects people who need home cleaning and care services with independent service professionals ("pros"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile phone number and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Request details: the service you ask for, your ZIP code, timing, frequency, job details and notes you type.
  • Messages: messages you exchange with a pro after the pro accepts your request.
  • Pros: business name, services, service areas, bio, reviews, and billing records. Pros pay a lead fee by card; card details are collected and stored by Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Text-message consent: whether you opted in to texts and when.
  • Technical data: basic device and log information, and a small amount of browser storage used to keep you signed in and save an unfinished request.

How we use it

  • To match your request with up to five pros and show you their profiles and reviews.
  • To let pros accept or decline your request and to let you and an accepting pro message each other.
  • To process lead fees, prevent fraud and keep the service secure.
  • To send service messages by email and, only if you opt in, by text (see below).
  • To improve the site and comply with the law.

Who sees your information

  • Pros: before accepting, a pro sees your first name, ZIP code and request details. Your full name, email address and phone number are shown only to a pro who accepts your request.
  • Service providers that run the site for us: our hosting provider (Netlify), database and authentication provider (Supabase), payment processor (Stripe), and messaging provider (GoHighLevel) for texts and related service messages.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging

If you opt in, we text you about your requests, pro responses, quotes and account activity. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information shared with providers that deliver our messages is used only to deliver them. Reply STOP to any text to opt out at any time. See our Terms and the text messaging page for the full program details.

Retention and security

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed for legal, tax and dispute purposes. We use encryption in transit, access controls and row-level database security. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

You can update your name and phone number in your account. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, and California residents may also ask what we collect and request that we not sell or share it (we do neither). Email us at and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our service is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
