Text messaging program

Home Key Clean service notifications

Home Key Clean can text you about your cleaning requests so you do not miss a reply. Texting is optional. You can use the full site without it.

How you opt in

When you create an account or submit a request on HomeKeyClean.com, you enter your mobile number and can check the box below. It is unchecked by default and you must check it yourself. This is the exact wording shown on our forms:

Example only. The live form is on the request page and the pro sign-up page.

What we text

  • Clients: a pro accepted your request, a new message from a pro, account notices.
  • Pros: a new request is waiting for your review, a new message from a client, account notices.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts, and we never sell or share your number for marketing.

Sample messages

  • Home Key Clean: A pro has accepted your Residential cleaning request. View details and message them at https://homekeyclean.com/account/ Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
  • Home Key Clean: New request for Residential cleaning in ZIP 90745. Review and accept or decline at https://homekeyclean.com/pro/ Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Home Key Clean: You have a new message about your cleaning request. Log in to reply: https://homekeyclean.com/account/ Reply STOP to opt out.

Frequency, cost and opting out
