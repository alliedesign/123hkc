Common questions

Why clean the dryer vent?

Lint buildup slows drying and can be a fire risk, so regular cleaning is a good habit.

How much does it cost?

Home Key Clean doesn’t set prices. Each pro is an independent contractor who quotes you directly, so you can compare the pros you hear from and choose.

How many pros will I hear from?

Up to 5 independent pros. Requests rotate across our pros so the work is shared fairly.
