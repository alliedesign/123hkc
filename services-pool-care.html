How it works

  1. Tell us what you need

    Answer a few questions about the job. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Get up to 5 options

    Pros take turns receiving requests, so the work is shared fairly and you hear from pros who are available.

  3. Choose your pro

    Chat with the pros, agree on a price directly, and book the one you like.

Common questions

What kinds of pools can pros service?

In-ground pools, above-ground pools and spas are commonly serviced. Tell us which you have in your request.

How much does it cost?

Home Key Clean doesn’t set prices. Each pro is an independent contractor who quotes you directly, so you can compare the pros you hear from and choose.

How many pros will I hear from?

Up to 5 independent pros. Requests rotate across our pros so the work is shared fairly.
